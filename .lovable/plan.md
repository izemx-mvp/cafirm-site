# CAFIRM website

## What to build
- Replace the placeholder home page with a polished, French-language CAFIRM consulting website using the supplied blue and white logos.
- Create the requested pages: home, cabinet, services, four service details, Morocco investment, news, six articles, appointment, contact, legal notices, privacy policy, and not-found.
- Add a shared responsive header/footer, mobile navigation, appointment and WhatsApp calls to action, client-side forms, FAQs, search and category filters, and the specified French content.
- Establish the navy, ivory, and restrained gold visual system, Cormorant Garamond/Inter typography, subtle Moroccan star pattern, motion, supplied branding, and favicon.
- Add page-specific metadata, structured data where appropriate, and crawler files.

## Technical approach
- Use TanStack Start's existing file-based routing and React/Tailwind setup; no backend or persistent storage.
- Keep editable business details, service copy, and article content in focused data/config files; use one shared template for service details and one for articles.
- Validate all content routes and metadata, responsive navigation, forms, links, and preview output before finishing.

## Assumptions
- Use the plausible firm details and sample testimonials requested in the brief as editable presentation content.
- Forms provide an on-screen confirmation and offer a prefilled WhatsApp handoff; they do not transmit or store submissions.
